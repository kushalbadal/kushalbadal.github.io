Journal of Computer Science and Technology2025

Novel Probabilistic and Machine Learning Approaches for the Protein Scaffold Gap Filling Problem

K. Badal, L. Qingge, X. Liu, B. Zhu

In de novo protein sequencing, we obtain incomplete protein sequences (scaffolds) from tandem mass spectrometry. We develop ML models using random forest, k-nearest neighbors, decision trees, and fully connected neural networks. For known mass gaps, we design a novel probabilistic algorithm achieving state-of-the-art performance.
Machine LearningBioinformaticsNeural Networks
IEEE Access2025

Attention-Enhanced CNN for High-Performance Deepfake Detection: A Multi-Dataset Study

S. Dasgupta, K. Badal, S. Chittam, T. Alam, K. Roy

We combine multi-head self-attention (MHSA) with a custom-designed CNN achieving 97% detection accuracy with 99.58 AUC. Cross-dataset performance: 98% on 140K Real and Fake Faces, 94% on Celeb-DF v2.
Deep LearningAttentionDeepfake Detection
